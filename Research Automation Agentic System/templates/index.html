Research Automation
Agentic System

As AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude become deeply embedded in daily workflows, many people have stopped doing deep, independent research — relying instead on single-turn LLM responses that may be outdated, shallow, or hallucinated. I built this Research Agent to counter that trend: a system that actively goes out, retrieves current information from multiple authoritative sources, synthesises evidence, and produces structured, verifiable outputs. It keeps me up to date with the latest trends in AI and data science without sacrificing the rigour of real research.

The agent runs a full multi-step pipeline — planning the research strategy, searching the web via Tavily, querying arXiv for academic preprints, and consulting Wikipedia for background context — before passing all findings to a dedicated writer and editor agent to produce a polished, citation-rich academic report.

Enter your research topic below. The more specific your prompt, the better the output — try including 3–4 details such as a target domain, a time frame, or a specific angle.

To inspect what the agent is doing at each stage, click the ➕ icon next to any pipeline step. The generated report includes clickable links in the references section — right-click to open in a new tab.
